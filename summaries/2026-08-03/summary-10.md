Comprehensive Summary — DND Session Notes
-----------------------------------------

### The Party Becomes Gatekeepers of the Keystone Doors

*   After the previous discussion with the gathered gods, the party receives confirmation that they have become **gatekeepers of the keystones**.
    
*   Kit Anger formally welcomes Gerk as:
    
    *   An **honorary member of the Narvee**.
        
    *   A **child of the tree**.
        
*   Kit Anger physically acknowledges Gerk with a powerful slap on the back, symbolizing acceptance.
    

### The Gods' Council Ends

*   Narul questions whether the gods can finally leave after the party has finished questioning them.
    
*   The party realizes that the gods were only brought together because of an accidental summoning.
    
*   Narul explains that:
    
    *   The party now has a greater responsibility.
        
    *   They are not merely "doormen" but **gatekeepers**.
        
    *   There is an important distinction:
        
        *   Gerk is the "door man" because of his personal ability.
            
        *   The rest of the party are "gatekeepers" because they hold the authority of the keystones.
            

### Gerk Demonstrates Keystone Ability

*   Plymouth encourages Gerk to demonstrate his new ability.
    
*   Gerk reaches forward and mimics opening a door.
    
*   Reality itself bends:
    
    *   An invisible door handle appears.
        
    *   Gerk pulls open a seamless doorway through reality.
        
    *   The doorway opens onto a beach at sunset somewhere on Midgard.
        
    *   The party hears gulls, smells salt water, and feels the ocean breeze.
        
*   The door does not create a visible portal; instead, reality itself folds open like a door.
    
*   Plymouth is visibly surprised and impressed.
    
*   Gerk asks whether he can choose where the door goes.
    
*   The response:
    
    *   The ability is poorly understood.
        
    *   The tomes being used describe the process but do not fully explain it.
        
    *   The destination appears to have been somewhere on Midgard, but the exact location is unknown.
        
*   The ability seems tied specifically to Gerk's role as the "door man."
    

### Other Party Members Attempt Keystone Interaction

*   Sylan attempts to use the keystone ability.
    
*   Holding the keystone, he reaches out into reality.
    
*   He feels something briefly:
    
    *   His hand brushes against something unseen.
        
    *   The sensation disappears.
        
    *   His hand returns empty.
        
*   Sylan concludes that there is something there but believes he needs more time to understand and control it.
    
*   The party learns that the ability may require practice and familiarity.
    

### The Gods Depart

*   The party agrees to release the gathered gods.
    
*   Vecna leaves casually:
    
    *   Says he needs to find a book.
        
    *   Tells the party to enjoy their new abilities.
        
    *   Reminds them they are still mortal and can die.
        
*   Vecna leaves with a warning not to get themselves killed.
    
*   Ben opens a doorway into his traveling tavern.
    
    *   The sound of a lively tavern comes through.
        
    *   He welcomes the party to visit whenever they wish.
        
    *   He hints they will see him again sooner for some than others.
        
*   Mahali follows Ben into the tavern.
    
    *   Invites the party back to the stronghold.
        
    *   Mentions preparing:
        
        *   Lamb soufflés.
            
        *   Cabbage stew.
            
        *   Cherry or lemon curd dessert.
            
*   Narul gives the party a respectful farewell:
    
    *   A nod.
        
    *   A gesture touching his forehead.
        
    *   A slight bow before leaving.
        

### Emet's Warning and Advice

*   Emet begins leaving but pauses to ask whether the party needs anything.
    
*   Dana says she believes her questions have been answered but will pray for further guidance.
    
*   Emet gives a final warning:
    
    *   "If the mind walks, hold your breath. Do not yell."
        
    *   "Trust the path, gatekeeper."
        
    *   "You now all hold the keys."
        
*   Emet leaves, leaving only the Raven Queen behind.

The Raven Queen's Judgment
==========================

*   Raven Queen approaches the party after observing the entire gathering.
    
*   Her appearance initially seems monstrous:
    
    *   A harpy-like figure.
        
    *   Covered in black glossy feathers.
        
    *   Wearing a white mask.
        
*   As she approaches, the illusion changes:
    
    *   The feathers become silky fabric.
        
    *   The monstrous appearance resolves into a regal humanoid figure.
        
*   Final appearance:
    
    *   Slight female figure.
        
    *   Around 5'6"–5'8".
        
    *   Dark flowing robes.
        
    *   White porcelain mask.
        
    *   Mask resembles delicate owl-like features.
        
    *   The robe and mask create an elegant, unnatural presence.
        

### Raven Queen's Message

*   The Raven Queen acknowledges that the party has opinions about her.
    
*   She recognizes:
    
    *   Some respect her.
        
    *   Others do not.
        
*   She says she cannot judge mortals by the same standards as gods because she remembers being mortal herself.
    
*   She warns:
    
    *   If she had known what she knows now, she would have acted differently against them.
        
    *   She is still capable of moving against them if necessary.
        

### Knowledge of the Party

*   She states:
    
    *   She knows where the party came from.
        
    *   She has walked those places herself.
        
    *   She has seen some of them on the Everfalls.
        
*   She specifically focuses her attention on:
    
    *   Sylan.
        
    *   Dana.
        
*   She says the party has exceeded her expectations.
    

### Her Assessment of the Party

*   She expected Silent to succeed and never doubted him.
    
*   She was less certain about the others.
    
*   She specifically examines Walker and Ord:
    
    *   Says she did not see this outcome coming.
        
    *   They have not impressed her beyond what she has seen from mortals.
        
    *   However, they have shown her something more than most mortals do.
        

### The Raven Queen's Offer

*   The Raven Queen offers to guide the party where they need to go.
    
*   However:
    
    *   She warns them not to make the journey foolish.
        
    *   She will not tolerate wasted time.
        
    *   Their lives and deaths have meaning.
        
*   She warns:
    
    *   Death is not something to casually play with.
        
    *   Some of them have limited lives remaining.
        
*   Her final message:
    
    *   She is not telling them to defeat or defy death.
        
    *   She is telling them to make their lives meaningful.
        
    *   "Make it something to remember."
