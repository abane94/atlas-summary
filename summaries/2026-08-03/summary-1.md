Comprehensive Summary
---------------------

### Gerk's Possible Role in the Mother's Prayer

*   While waiting during the ongoing council of gods, **Sylan** decides to reveal to **Plymouth Gawater** and the others that he believes **Gerk** is the child described in an old journal he previously discovered.
    
*   Sylan explains that:
    
    *   The journal described a child whose **mother died but the child survived**, and that the **Navi** were searching for this child.
        
    *   Initially he believed the search was hostile, so he kept the information secret while investigating.
        
    *   During a vision/meditation in **Jotunheim** (following the party's journey there), he learned that the child is **Gerk**.
        
    *   He believes Gerk is a **Keeper** who will ultimately **open the doors** (presumably the realm gates connected to the Key Stones).
        
*   Plymouth questions Sylan carefully about the journal:
    
    *   Whether it was handwritten.
        
    *   Whether it had multiple authors.
        
    *   Whether it could be dated historically.
        
    *   Whether it contained an explicit prophecy.
        
*   Sylan explains:
    
    *   The journal appeared relatively recent.
        
    *   One of its predictions—the plot against his family—came true.
        
    *   It did **not** explicitly contain a prophecy, only references to the mysterious child.
        

### The Mother's Prayer

*   Plymouth recognizes the description of the journal and asks whether it relates to something known as **"The Mother's Prayer"** (also called **"The Mother's Prophecy"**).
    
*   Dana recalls the prophetic verse that previously echoed throughout the Stronghold:
    
    > _The gate will open, whether by luck, skill, or blunder..._  
    > _...For watch as the eight are torn asunder..._  
    > _...The arches but doors waiting to be awakened..._
    
*   Plymouth confirms this prophecy is known as **The Mother's Prayer / Mother's Prophecy**.
    
*   After hearing Sylan's explanation, Plymouth understands Sylan's conclusion:
    
    *   **Gerk is believed to be the child referenced in the prophecy.**
        
    *   **Gerk may be the Keeper destined to open the doors.**
        

### Plymouth Examines Gerk

*   Plymouth becomes extremely interested in Gerk after hearing Sylan's theory.
    
*   **Kit Anger** also begins paying close attention to Gerk once the discussion turns toward the prophecy.
    
*   Plymouth approaches Gerk, asks him to kneel, and performs a careful physical examination, apparently searching for evidence that Gerk may truly be the prophesied individual.
    
*   Plymouth repeatedly emphasizes that he is not claiming the prophecy is true, but wants Gerk himself to understand that many events may already have been set in motion without his knowledge.
