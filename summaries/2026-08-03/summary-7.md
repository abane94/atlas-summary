Comprehensive Summary
---------------------

### Keystone assignments continue

*   The party continues formally accepting stewardship over the remaining **Keystones**, with Plymouth presenting each one based on each character's background or connection to a realm.
    
*   Plymouth explains that the previous Keepers were responsible only for **hiding and preserving** the Keystones, but the party's generation has a greater responsibility:
    
    *   **Protect** the realms.
        
    *   **Actively use** the Keystones.
        
    *   **Help their assigned realms grow and prosper.**
        

### Gerk becomes Keeper of Midgard

*   Gerk chooses and accepts the **Keystone of Midgard**.
    
*   Plymouth acknowledges that Gerk has earned it by discovering and collecting the Keystones.
    
*   Upon accepting it:
    
    *   A mysterious breeze fills the cavern.
        
    *   White flower petals appear.
        
    *   Gerk again sees the mysterious girl/woman who has appeared to him before, walking away across the central expanse while leaving a trail of white flowers.
        
*   This time **Sylan and the rest of the party can also see her**, confirming she is not merely a vision unique to Gerk.
    
*   Gerk briefly suspects everyone is only pretending to see her to humor him. A successful Perception check reveals Plymouth is initially agreeing simply to calm Gerk, but Plymouth ultimately confirms that he genuinely sees her as well.
    
*   Gerk wants to immediately follow her, but is convinced to finish the Keystone ceremony first.
    

### Sylan becomes Keeper of Gilbrick

*   Plymouth offers **Gilbrick's Keystone** to Sylan because he is a child of that realm's lord.
    
*   Sylan accepts responsibility for protecting Gilbrick.
    

### Walker chooses the Reach over Lorel

*   Plymouth initially offers Walker the **Keystone of Lorel**, his homeland.
    
*   Walker reflects that although Lorel is where he came from, it no longer represents who he has become.
    
*   Walker mentally consults Glimmen, who reminds him that "home is where we make it" and supports whichever choice Walker makes.
    
*   Walker ultimately decides that **the Reach** better represents his current life and purpose.
    
*   Plymouth accepts the decision and instead entrusts Walker with the **Keystone of the Reach**, noting that its vast size suits Walker and Glimmen's ability to travel.
    

### Dana becomes Keeper of Patel

*   Plymouth remembers Dana previously wanting easier access to **Patel** because of its unique relationship with time.
    
*   Rather than simply granting access, he offers Dana stewardship of the realm itself.
    
*   Dana gratefully accepts, stating she would be honored to **guard the center of the Rings of Atlas**.
    
*   Plymouth returns the gesture with a respectful bow.
    

### Ord chooses Feldspar

*   Remaining available realms are **Feldspar**, **Lorel**, **Turof**, and **Lith**.
    
*   Plymouth notes that unlike the others, Ord does not have an obvious personal connection directing his choice.
    
*   Ord briefly asks whether Gilbrick is still available before learning it has already been claimed.
    
*   After discussing Feldspar and Lith:
    
    *   Feldspar is described as a realm of **magic, science, alchemy, and experimentation**.
        
    *   Plymouth clarifies that Lith has rejected conventional magic but is not ignorant; its people preserve folklore, cryptids, fairy tales, and wonder.
        
*   Ord chooses to become Keeper of **Feldspar**.
