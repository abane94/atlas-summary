Comprehensive Summary
---------------------

### Important Plot Points

*   The party remains in the ongoing council of gods, where time passes differently for divine beings. The gods are content to remain indefinitely while discussions continue, and the party is free to speak without worrying about wasting their time.
    
*   Gerk decides to reveal to the group that Sylan believes he is a "chosen" or otherwise significant individual.
    
*   Sylan explains to Plymouth and the others that:
    
    *   Years ago he found a handwritten journal.
        
    *   The journal mentioned that the **Navi** were searching for a child whose **mother had died but the child had survived**.
        
    *   During a meditation in **Jotunheim**, he received a revelation identifying **Gerk** as that child.
        
    *   Based on that vision, Sylan believes Gerk is connected to the **Keepers** and will one day **open the doors**.
        
*   Plymouth takes the revelation seriously and begins carefully questioning Sylan about the journal:
    
    *   Whether it was handwritten.
        
    *   Whether multiple authors contributed to it.
        
    *   Whether it contained dates or historical references.
        
    *   Whether it contained any explicit prophecy.
        
*   Sylan explains:
    
    *   The journal appeared relatively recent.
        
    *   One of its other entries described a plot to murder his family, which later came true.
        
    *   It did **not** explicitly contain a prophecy—only references to the child and the attack on his family.
        
*   Plymouth reveals that he recognizes the journal by reputation and asks whether Sylan has heard of something called **"The Mother's Prayer."** Sylan has not.
