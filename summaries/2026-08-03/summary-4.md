Comprehensive Summary
---------------------

*   **Plymouth** invited the rest of the party to formally join **Gerk** as part of whatever destiny has been revealed about him. While Gerk is considered a direct "child of the Great Tree," the rest of the party would instead become something akin to brothers and sisters of Gerk or members of the same greater family tied to the Great Tree.
    
*   Walker, through his connection with **Glimmen**, privately admitted that he had no idea what Plymouth meant by the "Great Tree," showing that this revelation is new even to those with significant knowledge.
    
*   The party generally agreed to stand with Gerk despite not fully understanding what they were agreeing to. Their loyalty to Gerk outweighed the lack of information.
    
*   **Dana** reaffirmed that she trusts Gerk's destiny, believing that if higher powers have chosen him then there must be a purpose behind it.
    
*   Discussion returned to **Daisy**, the mysterious little blonde girl only Gerk appears to interact with.
    
    *   Most characters still cannot perceive Daisy.
        
    *   Gerk reiterated that Daisy has demonstrated impossible abilities, including apparently stopping time.
        
    *   Dana confirmed that the unexplained flowers Gerk previously received are real evidence that Daisy was physically present, since flowers appeared where none should have existed.
        
    *   Plymouth acknowledged this as troubling and stated he would need to investigate further, implying Daisy is not something he currently understands.
        
*   Every member of the party ultimately stepped forward to participate in Plymouth's ceremony alongside Gerk, officially choosing to follow whatever role Gerk has been selected for.
