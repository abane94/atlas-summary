### Important Plot Points / Lore Confirmed

#### Gods and Mortals

*   **Major gods (Voyager Gods and Elder Gods) cannot directly interfere with mortal affairs.**
    
*   This restriction explains why powerful beings often require mortal agents.
    
*   Because of this, the party believes:
    
    *   **Grixto is likely acting as Kar's mortal proxy**, allowing Kar to influence Atlas indirectly.
        

#### Kar

*   Additional clarification from the DM:
    
    *   **Kar is NOT technically a god.**
        
    *   Kar is an extremely powerful entity, more comparable to a **demon/devil-like being** than a deity.
        
    *   Because he is **not** one of the Elder/Voyager Gods, he **can** directly interact with mortals.
        
*   Kar resides within the **Astral Sea**, which in Atlas is the same location as the **Abyssal Plane**.
    

#### Nevarr

*   The prevailing belief among the gods is that **Nevarr somehow wrote himself into one of his books** after authoring so many.
    
*   It is theorized that:
    
    *   He may literally exist within his writings.
        
    *   Recovering enough of his books could potentially provide a way to reach him.
        
*   This was discussed as a possible future side quest.
    

#### Raven Queen's Origin

The party compared two versions of the Raven Queen's ascension:

**Raven Queen's version**

*   She was originally a mortal elf named **Nira**.
    
*   She died during a battle.
    
*   Upon reaching the Everfalls she encountered Narul.
    
*   Narul offered her a chance to continue existing.
    
*   She accepted and learned to harbor souls.
    
*   Eventually she overthrew Narul.
    
*   She discovered gods cannot truly be killed.
    
*   Rather than destroy Narul, she left him in the weakened state where the party eventually found him.
    

**Vecna's version**

*   Similar overall story.
    
*   Claims she was **assassinated**, not killed in battle.
    
*   Portrays her actions in a much more manipulative and self-serving light.
    

The party recognizes both accounts likely contain bias.
