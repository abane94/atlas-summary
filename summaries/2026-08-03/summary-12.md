Comprehensive Summary — Portal Keystone Testing and Return to the Stronghold Area
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   After speaking with the mysterious harpy-like servant of the Raven Queen, the party remained behind with several important NPCs:
    
    *   Glimond (Walker's dragon)
        
    *   Juten Prime
        
    *   Elris
        
    *   Raiden
        
    *   Boris
        
    *   Kit Anger
        
*   The Raven Queen gave Sylan (Triple Champ’s character) a final message before leaving:
    
    *   She encouraged him not to give up on life or prepare himself for death.
        
    *   She reminded him to remember where he has been, where he is going, and what he still has.
        
    *   She told him he had been given a weapon and was a "talon" of her order, not simply a butcher or mindless killer.
        
    *   She transformed from her disguised form into a harpy-like being with giant black wings and departed.
        
*   The party began experimenting with the newly acquired ability to open portals.
    
    *   Gerk attempted to open a portal, but the destination appeared to be random.
        
    *   The first portal opened to:
        
        *   A gray mountainside.
            
        *   Rain falling across a stone dais.
            
        *   Distant thunder from a storm far away.
            
    *   The group realized Gerk's portal ability may not be fully controlled yet.
        
*   The party theorized that practice and familiarity with locations may improve portal accuracy.
    
    *   Dana suggested focusing on a known place, such as the Stronghold's Great Hall.
        
    *   Gerk attempted again, rolling a check.
        
    *   His result was debated as either a 9 or 14 depending on bonuses.
        
*   The second portal opened into:
    
    *   A dark forest at night.
        
    *   Glowing insects/fireflies moved between the trees.
        
    *   The area appeared peaceful and quiet.
        
    *   Dana recognized the environment as likely being the same type of forest surrounding Langdale and the Stronghold.
        
*   Walker immediately attempted to identify the area due to his Wanderer background and his past travels.
    
    *   He rolled Nature.
        
    *   The DM confirmed:
        
        *   The forest contained the same flora and fauna as the area around Langdale.
            
        *   Walker recognized a specific old, oddly angled tree from a previous bee-related adventure.
            
        *   The location was somewhere near Langdale.
            
*   Walker attempted to immediately walk through the portal with Glimond and nearby NPCs.
    
    *   The party stopped him, concerned about rushing through an unknown portal.
        
    *   The group discussed that Walker has already had dangerous portal experiences and should not immediately jump through.
        
*   The discovery created a major realization:
    
    *   Gerk may now possess the ability to travel between locations.
        
    *   The party recognized this as a significant responsibility and power.
        
    *   Sylan suggested the group organize themselves and think through the implications rather than immediately exploring.
        
*   Plymouth Gawater (referred to during the conversation) became interested in understanding the portal ability.
    
    *   Walker jokingly claimed the device/ability was called "Nunya."
        
    *   Plymouth misunderstood and asked if it was some kind of technology.
        
    *   The group clarified it was a joke ("none ya business").
        
    *   Plymouth remained interested in the ability, mentioning that knowing how it works could allow travel to other ports/locations.
        
*   Dana became overwhelmed by the situation and asked Kit Anger for alcohol.
    
    *   Kit provided a bottle from his supplies.
        
    *   The party prepared to discuss the implications of their new portal capability and what locations they might access.
