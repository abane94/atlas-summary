Comprehensive Summary — The Raven Queen’s Judgment and the Keystone Door
------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   The party finished their interaction with the gathered deities and powerful beings following the recent trials/keystone events.
    
*   Ben departed first:
    
    *   He opened a doorway into his traveling tavern from thin air.
        
    *   He invited the party to visit whenever they wished.
        
    *   He and Mahali left together, with Mahali mentioning food waiting at the stronghold (lamb soufflé, cabbage stew, and cherry/lemon curd).
        
*   Narul gave the party a quiet farewell:
    
    *   He acknowledged the group before leaving.
        
    *   His departure was respectful and subdued, implying continued awareness of the party’s actions.
        
*   Met was preparing to leave and asked if the party needed anything before his departure.
    
    *   Dana said her questions had been answered and she would seek further guidance through prayer if needed.
        
    *   Met gave the party a final warning:
        
        *   “If the mind walks, hold your breath. Do not yell.”
            
        *   “Trust the path gatekeeper.”
            
        *   “You now all hold the keys.”
            
    *   Met then left, leaving the Raven Queen as the final deity present.
        

### The Raven Queen’s Message

*   Raven Queen approached the party.
    
*   Her appearance shifted as she approached:
    
    *   Initially appearing as a harpy-like creature covered in black glossy feathers.
        
    *   The image gradually clarified into a regal, humanoid feminine figure.
        
    *   She wore a dark flowing robe and a white porcelain mask with owl/feather-like design elements.
        
*   She studied each party member carefully before speaking.
    

### The Raven Queen’s Judgment

*   The Raven Queen acknowledged that the party had opinions about her, some positive and some negative.
    
*   She stated that:
    
    *   Mortals make mistakes and she does not hold their past actions against them entirely.
        
    *   However, if she had known what she knows now, she might have acted differently toward them.
        
*   She recognized that the party had exceeded her expectations:
    
    *   She did not initially believe they were capable of accomplishing what they had.
        
    *   She specifically noted that some individuals, such as Sylan, were predictable in their determination.
        
    *   She was surprised by others, particularly Walker and Ord.
        
*   She did not consider the party exceptional compared to all mortals, but she acknowledged they had shown something beyond what most mortals demonstrate.
    

### The Raven Queen’s Offer and Warning

*   The Raven Queen agreed to lead the party where they needed to go.
    
*   She warned them:
    
    *   Do not turn their journey into a fool’s journey.
        
    *   Do not waste her time or their own.
        
    *   Death is not something to treat casually.
        
*   She implied that:
    
    *   Some members of the party have limited chances remaining.
        
    *   Some have already spent portions of whatever chances they had.
        
*   Her advice was not to avoid death entirely, but to make their lives meaningful:
    
    *   “Make it a good one.”
        
    *   “Make it something to remember.”
        

### Message to Sylan

*   Sylan declined to ask anything further from the Raven Queen, stating that she had already done enough for him.
    
*   The Raven Queen accepted that choice:
    
    *   She told him to remember where he had been and where he was going.
        
    *   Remember what he has.
        
    *   Do not throw it away.
        
*   She warned that no one knows when death will come.
    
*   She told Sylan to follow the path he had been given.
    
*   She described him as:
    
    *   “A talon of my order.”
        
    *   Not a butcher.
        
    *   A refined instrument.
        

### Departure of the Raven Queen

*   The Raven Queen transformed back into her harpy-like form:
    
    *   Black wings emerged.
        
    *   Shadows clung to her feathers.
        
*   She flew away, leaving the party with:
    
    *   Glimmen
        
    *   Juten Prime
        
    *   Elris
        
    *   Araden
        
    *   Boris
        
    *   Kit Anger

The Keystone Door Experiment
----------------------------

*   After the Raven Queen left, the party discussed where to go next.
    
*   Gerk experimented with opening the keystone door.
    
*   The group joked about wanting a “beach episode,” but Gerk’s first attempt did not lead there.
    

### First Door Attempt

*   Gerk attempted to focus on a destination.
    
*   The party discussed whether the ability depended on:
    
    *   Familiar locations.
        
    *   Focus.
        
    *   Practice.
        
*   The door opened to:
    
    *   A gray mountainside.
        
    *   Rain falling across a stone dais.
        
    *   Thunder from a distant storm.
        
*   The party concluded the door does not always open exactly where intended.
    

### Second Door Attempt

*   Gerk tried again, focusing on the stronghold.
    
*   He rolled a check:
    
    *   Initial result: 9.
        
    *   Possible adjusted result: 14 with bonuses.
        
*   The door opened again.
    

### Second Destination

*   The door revealed:
    
    *   A quiet, dark forest.
        
    *   Moonlight filtering through trees.
        
    *   Fireflies/lightning bugs moving through the woods.
        
    *   The peaceful ambient sounds of a resting forest.
        
*   The party had not yet determined whether to enter or investigate further.
