Comprehensive Session Summary (Plot-Focused)
--------------------------------------------

### The Party Becomes the New Keepers of the Keystone Realms

The party completed the selection of the remaining keystones and formally accepted the responsibility of becoming **keepers/gatekeepers** of the realms. Plymouth Gawater explained that each member would take responsibility for protecting one of the realms connected by the keystones.

The current distribution of keystones became:

*   **Burke** — Midgard
    
*   **Sylan** — the realm of his god Gilbrick
    
*   **Dana** — Patel / the Core
    
*   **Walker** — The Reach
    
*   **Ord** — Feldspar, the second most inner sanctum of knowledge, magic, and alchemical experimentation
    
*   **Ciri** — Lith
    

Three keystones had apparently already been selected, leaving Ciri as the final party member to choose. Ciri selected **Lith**, despite its reputation for rejecting or denying magic.

* * *

### Lith Keystone Choice

Plymouth explained that Lith is a realm where the people have sworn away magic, but this does not mean magic is absent. Instead:

*   Magic exists but is misunderstood.
    
*   Magical phenomena are treated as folklore, fairy tales, legends, or cryptids.
    
*   Strange creatures and events from other realms sometimes leak into Lith and become myths.
    
*   A magical being appearing there could become the origin of a local legend.
    

Plymouth warned Ciri that the people of Lith may call her a witch if she openly uses magic, but she will still become a significant figure in their history.

Ciri accepted the responsibility and received the Lith keystone.

* * *

### Two Forgotten Keystone Realms Remain Without Keepers

Plymouth revealed a somber truth:

*   There are **eight keystones**, but only six current party members.
    
*   Originally, there were eight members involved in the quest before the number changed.
    
*   Two keystones remain without assigned keepers.
    

Plymouth stated that these two realms should not be forgotten:

*   **Terov**
    
*   **Lorel**
    

Kit Anger and Plymouth will act as temporary stewards of those realms until others can take up the mantle.

* * *

### The Party Learns Their New Role

Plymouth explained that by accepting the keystones, the party has become more than simply adventurers.

They are now:

*   **Keepers of the Keystone realms**
    
*   Protectors of powerful artifacts
    
*   Responsible for ensuring the keystones do not fall into dangerous hands
    

This creates an interesting reversal:

The party originally came seeking information about the lich pursuing the keystones. Instead, they have now become the guardians of the very things that the lich wants.

Plymouth acknowledged that fate seems to have placed them in a difficult position:

> They came looking for protection from the threat, but instead became responsible for protecting the things the threat seeks.

He could not dictate how the party uses the keystones, but offered guidance.

* * *

### Gerk Becomes a Child of the Tree

The newest member of the group, Gerk, received an additional revelation.

By accepting the mantle, Gerk became:

*   An honorary member of the **Narvee**
    
*   A **Child of the Tree**
    

Kit Anger welcomed him into the group with a powerful slap on the back.

The party joked about Gerk being called a “doorman” instead of a gatekeeper, but Plymouth clarified:

*   Gerk is specifically the **doorman**
    
*   The others are **gatekeepers**
    

This distinction appears meaningful, with Gerk having a unique ability related to opening paths between places.

* * *

### Gerk Demonstrates the Door Ability

Plymouth instructed Gerk to mimic opening a door.

When Gerk reached forward:

*   His hand found an invisible door handle.
    
*   He opened a doorway through reality itself.
    
*   The opening seamlessly peeled back reality without a visible threshold.
    
*   The doorway led to a beach at sunset on Midgard.
    
*   The party heard gulls, smelled salt air, and felt ocean breeze.
    

Plymouth admitted he did not expect the ability to work immediately.

The ability appears to allow Gerk to create portals/doors between locations, though the exact control and limitations are unclear.

Gerk asked whether he could choose where doors went. Plymouth explained that he was working from ancient texts and did not fully understand the ability yet. The first door appeared to lead somewhere on Midgard, specifically a coastline.

* * *

### Sylan Tests His Keystone

Sylan attempted to use his keystone.

He reached forward while holding it and felt as if he brushed against something hidden, but the sensation disappeared.

Sylan concluded that he may need more time to understand and master the keystone.

Plymouth acknowledged that the party members are truly powerful now.

* * *

### Meeting With the Gods Ends

After the revelations, the party prepared to dismiss the gathered gods.

Gerk joked that Plymouth had summoned the gods accidentally and was now keeping them around.

Plymouth clarified that the situation was mostly caused by Sylan, who had identified Gerk as:

*   The Child of the Tree
    
*   The subject of the mother's prayer
    

Walker asked if any of the gods had more information about **Grixto**.

No further information was provided.

Vecna responded sarcastically/positively, saying he needed to go find a book, implying he may search for information regarding Grixto.

The meeting ended with the gods leaving.
