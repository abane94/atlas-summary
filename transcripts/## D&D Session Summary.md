# Story / Lore Updates

## The Gods cannot directly interfere with mortals

One of the most important pieces of information reaffirmed.

* Elder Gods and Voyager Gods cannot directly interfere in mortal affairs.
* They require mortal agents to accomplish their goals.
* This makes it much more plausible that **Grixto** (transcription uncertain) is acting as Car's representative in the mortal world.

This is one of the biggest strategic revelations from the session.

---

## Car

Clarification from the DM.

Previous assumption:

> Car is a god.

Correction:

* Car is **NOT technically a god.**
* Car is an immensely powerful entity.
* The DM compared him more closely to:

  * a demon
  * a devil
  * another extremely powerful extraplanar being

Because he is **not** one of the true gods:

* he **can directly interact with mortals**
* unlike the Elder/Voyager Gods

This explains why Car is able to personally influence events.

---

## Divine Restrictions

The DM clarified exactly who is bound by the "cannot interfere" rule.

The restriction applies to:

* Elder Gods
* Voyager Gods

It does **not necessarily apply** to:

* Demigods
* Minor deities
* Other powerful supernatural beings

Those beings may still directly influence mortals.

# Raven Queen History

This was one of the largest lore discussions.

Two different versions of history now exist.

---

## Raven Queen's Version

According to the Raven Queen herself:

* She was originally a mortal elf.
* Her mortal name was **Nira**.
* She died in battle.
* Upon death she encountered **Narul**.
* Narul offered her a chance to continue existing.
* She accepted.
* Narul taught her how to harbor souls.
* Eventually she became powerful enough to overthrow Narul.
* Gods cannot truly be killed.
* Rather than destroy him completely, she left Narul in the weakened state in which the party eventually discovered him.

She described this almost as repayment:

> "You saved me, so I spared you."

---

## Vecna's Version

Vecna tells a noticeably different story.

Major differences include:

* Raven Queen was assassinated rather than dying in battle.
* Vecna portrays her in a much more manipulative and self-serving light.
* He implies she seduced or manipulated Narul in order to steal his power.

The exact truth remains uncertain.

---

## Important Difference

The clearest contradiction between the two stories:

Raven Queen:

* died in battle

Vecna:

* assassinated
