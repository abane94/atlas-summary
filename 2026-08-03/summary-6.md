Comprehensive Summary
=====================

Gerk is officially chosen as a Doorman of an Arch
-------------------------------------------------

*   The party steps forward to support **Gerk** as he accepts the responsibility laid before him.
    
*   **Kit Anger** and **Juten Prime** deliberately step back, recognizing that the events unfolding are significant and should proceed without interference.
    
*   Plymouth Gawater explains that Gerk is a **"son of the Great Tree"**, tying him directly to the ancient prophecy surrounding the Mother's Prayer.
    
*   Gerk is told that he will become the first **Doorman** in many years—guardian of one of the Arches and its corresponding Keystone.
    
*   A Doorman's responsibilities include:
    
    *   Guarding a specific Arch.
        
    *   Opening and closing access through it.
        
    *   Protecting the Keystone.
        
    *   Using its power responsibly to help its realm prosper.
        

The origin of the Keystones is revealed
---------------------------------------

Plymouth provides major lore about the creation of the Keystones.

*   The **Eldarin Tree** is an ancient, sacred tree revered by the Navi.
    
*   It is one of the oldest and most magically significant locations in Atlas.
    
*   The Keystones were created by studying and distilling the **rings of the Eldarin Tree**.
    
*   The rings of the tree mirror the "rings" or structure of Atlas itself.
    
*   Creating the Keystones unintentionally triggered **the Mother's Prayer**, beginning the prophecy that periodically chooses individuals to preserve the health of Atlas.
    
*   Gerk's selection is presented as part of this ancient prophecy rather than coincidence.
    

Gerk chooses the Keystone of Midgard
------------------------------------

*   Plymouth first presents Gerk with the Keystone of **Patel/Petal** (transcription uncertain), but explains that Gerk may choose whichever realm he wishes.
    
*   The party discusses several realms to help him decide.
    
*   Gerk ultimately chooses **Midgard's Keystone**.
    
*   Walker realizes he has already been carrying the Midgard Keystone and hands it over to Plymouth.
    
*   Plymouth formally entrusts Gerk with the Midgard Keystone.
    
*   Unlike previous Keepers, whose role was merely to hide and preserve the Keystone, Gerk is expected to:
    
    *   Protect it.
        
    *   Use it.
        
    *   Help make Midgard greater through it.
        

A mysterious vision accompanies Gerk's acceptance
-------------------------------------------------

Immediately after Gerk accepts Midgard's Keystone:

*   A breeze passes through the cavern.
    
*   White flower petals drift through the air.
    
*   Gerk sees the mysterious young girl he has encountered before.
    
*   She walks away through one of the massive corridors.
    
*   Rather than walking on solid ground, she appears to float while leaving a trail of white flowers.
    
*   Gerk asks if everyone else can see her.
    
*   Sylan and Walker immediately tell him they can.
    
*   Gerk suspects they may simply be humoring him.
    
*   A natural 20 Insight/Perception check reveals Plymouth is only agreeing to calm Gerk rather than confirming what he truly sees.
    
*   Plymouth refuses to elaborate, telling Gerk that following her is **his journey**.
    
*   The party convinces Gerk to wait until the ceremony is complete before pursuing her.
    

Sylan accepts responsibility for Gilbrick
-----------------------------------------

*   Plymouth offers **Sylan** the Keystone of **Gilbrick**, noting that as a child of that realm's lord he should have first choice.
    
*   Sylan briefly looks toward the others before accepting.
    
*   Sylan officially becomes the guardian associated with the Keystone of Gilbrick.
    

Walker is offered Lorel
-----------------------

*   Plymouth next approaches Walker.
    
*   He notes that Walker is the only member of the party who is not originally from Midgard.
    
*   Walker is offered the **Keystone of Lorel**, his home realm.
    
*   The transcript ends before Walker gives his answer.
