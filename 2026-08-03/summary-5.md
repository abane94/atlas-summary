Comprehensive Summary
=====================

*   The party continued discussing Gerk's strange experience and concluded that **Daisy almost certainly had been present**, as flowers (described as Daisy's signature) inexplicably appeared around Gerk.
    
*   All party members (Dana, Gerk, Walker, Ord, Sylan, and Ciri) were asked to step forward to participate in the ceremony surrounding Gerk's destiny.
    
*   After some joking about crowns—particularly Glimmen wanting one—the party unanimously agreed to stand with Gerk despite not fully understanding what they were committing to.
    
*   The assembled powerful figures, including Kit Anger, Juten Prime, Ben, Mahali, the Raven Queen, Vecna, Elrus, and Araden, largely stepped back and observed the ceremony without interfering.
    
*   Plymouth Gawater explained the true significance of Gerk's lineage:
    
    *   Gerk is the **"Son of the Great Tree."**
        
    *   The tree is the **Eldarin Tree**, an ancient sacred tree of the Navi people.
        
    *   The Eldarin Tree was the source of the knowledge used to create the **Keystones**.
        
    *   The Keystones were created by studying and distilling the tree's rings, unintentionally setting the **Mother's Prayer** into motion.
        
    *   The creation of the Keystones also initiated an ancient prophecy that periodically selects individuals to safeguard all of Atlas.
        
*   Plymouth explained that Gerk and the party were accepting responsibility under that prophecy—not merely to protect the tree, but to preserve the health and prosperity of all Atlas.
    
*   Gerk was formally offered the ancient title of **Doorman of one of the Arches**, a role that has apparently gone unfilled for many years.
    
    *   The Doorman is responsible for opening and closing an Arch.
        
    *   The Doorman protects that Arch and possesses the authority to connect with it through its Keystone.
        
*   Plymouth removed a Keystone directly from one of the concentric circles in the floor and presented Gerk with the **Keystone of Patel**, asking whether this was the Keystone he wished to hold.
    
*   Gerk realized he apparently has a choice of which realm's Keystone he will become responsible for, prompting the party to begin discussing the various realms and their characteristics before making a decision.
