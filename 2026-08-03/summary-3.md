Comprehensive Summary
---------------------

### Gerk and the Mother's Prayer

*   A member of the Navi questions **Gerk** extensively about his motivations, asking whether he truly wishes to continue following the path laid before him.
    
*   Gerk repeatedly answers that he is happiest simply:
    
    *   Smashing things.
        
    *   Traveling with his friends.
        
    *   Continuing what he's already been doing.
        
*   The Navi explains that the **Mother's Prayer** is an ancient prophecy whose origins are unknown. No one knows who first spoke it or who originally recorded it.
    
*   Numerous versions of the prophecy have been found throughout history, written by different authors. They are described as being like fruit growing from a single tree:
    
    *   **The Mother's Prayer** is the tree.
        
    *   Each chosen individual is one of its fruit.
        
*   The prophecy traditionally foretells:
    
    *   A catastrophic event.
        
    *   The rise of a hero.
        
    *   A series of increasingly difficult choices that hero must make.
        
*   Gerk is told that he has already made difficult choices, but that the hardest ones are still ahead and that **the time described in the prophecy is approaching quickly.**
    

### The Nature of the Chosen

*   The Navi explains that Gerk is believed to be one of the **sons or daughters of the Great Tree**, the chosen figures described by the Mother's Prayer.
    
*   This status does **not** mean his life was predetermined. Rather:
    
    *   The prophecy spoke of him.
        
    *   His choices have always remained his own.
        
*   The written "Mother's Tales" have even been observed to **rewrite themselves** if one of the chosen abandons their destined path.
    
*   The Navi states he has personally encountered **three previous chosen individuals**:
    
    *   One accepted their role.
        
    *   Two rejected or abandoned it.
        
*   There has **not been a willing chosen hero for approximately 1,500 years.**
    

### Gerk Accepts the Role

*   Gerk questions whether the prophecy could simply apply to many different people.
    
*   The Navi explains that recent events and specific details known only through Sylan's discovery strongly suggest the prophecy is referring to Gerk specifically.
    
*   Gerk ultimately agrees that if accepting the role will help Atlas, then he is willing to do so.
    
*   The Navi enthusiastically accepts Gerk's decision, stating that the Navi have waited centuries for someone to willingly take up the mantle.
    

### The Party's Role

*   After Gerk accepts, the Navi turns to the rest of the party.
    
*   They explain that joining Gerk is entirely voluntary.
    
*   If they choose to accompany him:
    
    *   They become his official companions in fulfilling the Mother's Prayer.
        
    *   They are symbolically treated as brothers and sisters alongside one of the children of the Great Tree.
        
*   Walker is confused by the terminology, as neither he nor Glimmen fully understand the significance of the Great Tree.
    
*   Most of the discussion devolves into jokes about crowns, capes, and familial titles.
    
*   Eventually:
    
    *   Dana agrees to continue following Gerk, saying that if destiny has chosen him then there must be a purpose.
        
    *   Ciri voices support from the shadows in typical fashion.
        
    *   The implication is that the party intends to remain with Gerk.
        

### Daisy

*   Gerk again brings up the mysterious little blonde girl **Daisy**.
    
*   He asks if anyone has finally figured out who she is.
    
*   The Navi admits they have **never seen Daisy** despite Gerk mentioning her several times.
    
*   Gerk reminds everyone that:
    
    *   Daisy previously stopped time.
        
    *   No one except him appeared to notice.
        
*   The scene ends with the mystery of Daisy remaining unresolved.
